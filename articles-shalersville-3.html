OCTOBER ZONING SHOCKWAVE: TRUSTEES ADVANCE HEAVY INDUSTRY REDEFINITIONS TO PERMANENTLY BLOCK BITDEER 750-MW AI CAMPUS

Published: October 2026 | Classification: Field Dossier & Municipal Intercept

The municipal resistance grid in Portage County has entered its most aggressive phase yet. Following relentless public pressure and standing-room-only hearings at Shalersville Town Hall, the legislative battle against corporate energy extraction has reached a boiling point. Shalersville Township Trustees and the Zoning Commission have officially pushed forward precision structural code amendments that legally wall off the township from speculative mega-scale artificial intelligence and cryptocurrency data center campuses.

// THE TARGET: BITDEER TECHNOLOGIES & GEIS COMPANIES 750-MW PROJECT

At the center of this regional showdown is a massive, high-voltage infrastructure proposal spearheaded by Singapore-based Bitdeer Technologies Group in active partnership with Geis Companies. The joint venture targeted over 150 acres of Geis-owned land strategically situated near the Ohio Turnpike to construct a sprawling 750-megawatt multi-building computing complex.

While corporate representatives attempted to pitch the multi-million dollar cash flow, local pushback exposed the hidden structural costs: massive electrical grid strain, high-volume water consumption, and the permanent transformation of a peaceful rural community into an industrial substation zone.

// THE LEGAL ARCHITECTURE: "HEAVY INDUSTRY" ZERO-STATE

To fundamentally checkmate the Bitdeer/Geis footprint, local officials utilized precision municipal code reclassifications:

  • Industrial Reclassification: Proposed massive data center operations have been explicitly carved out of lenient "Light Industry" definitions and reclassified under strict "Heavy Industry" standards.
  • The Zoning Zero-State: Shalersville Township currently maintains zero acres of land zoned for heavy industry. Consequently, under the newly enacted rules, the multi-building campus legally cannot fit or be permitted anywhere within township borders.
  • Moratorium Shield: The township's emergency moratorium on data centers remains fully locked down through the first week of November, with trustees prepared to extend it further.
// THE 10-DECIBEL NOISE FLASHPOINT: During tense planning sessions, Geis Companies representatives petitioned that their data center campus be granted permission to exceed Shalersville's baseline ambient noise limits by up to 10 decibels. Portage County Regional Planning Commission Director Todd Peetz and local homeowners fired back instantly, noting that a 10 dB bump represents a staggering ten-fold increase in physical sound intensity. Planners held a razor-sharp line: "They shouldn't be adding any noise to the neighbors. None. Zero."

// FIELD REPORTING & GRASSROOTS TRANSPARENCY

Independent field journalism—highlighted by investigative coverage from local outlets such as *The Portager*—has chronicled how ordinary residents have completely overwhelmed corporate PR efforts. From citizens spilling out of open town hall windows to meticulously presenting opposition conditions, the grassroots group *Shalersville Strong* has turned Portage County into a national blueprint for community self-defense.
