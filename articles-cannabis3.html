// MOLECULAR BREAKDOWN: CANNABINOIDS & PHYSIOLOGICAL BENEFITS

To understand what cannabis does for the human body, each compound must be examined by its specific binding affinities and biological effects:

1. Delta-9-THC (Tetrahydrocannabinol) — The Primary Agonist

What It Is: The principal psychoactive and therapeutic compound in cannabis. It directly binds to CB1 receptors in the brain and spinal cord.

  • Analgesia (Pain Relief): Interrupts chronic pain signals across neuropathic and inflammatory pathways without depressing respiratory functions.
  • Antiemetic & Appetite Stimulation: Suppresses nausea and vomiting in chemotherapy patients while counteracting severe muscle wasting (cachexia).
  • Neuroprotection: Acts as a powerful antioxidant in the brain, reducing neuroinflammation and protecting neurons against ischemia and trauma.
  • Intraocular Pressure Reduction: Relieves pressure in the eyes for glaucoma management.

2. CBD (Cannabidiol) — The Non-Intoxicating Neuro-Regulator

What It Is: A major non-psychoactive cannabinoid that acts as a negative allosteric modulator of CB1 receptors and an agonist at serotonin 5-HT1A receptors.

  • Anticonvulsant (Anti-Seizure): Calms hyper-excitable electrical activity in the brain, reducing grand mal seizures in intractable pediatric epilepsy.
  • Anxiolytic & Anti-Stress: Modulates serotonin receptors to relieve severe anxiety, PTSD, and panic disorders without physical addiction.
  • Anti-Inflammatory: Inhibits pro-inflammatory cytokines, making it effective for rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease, and gut inflammation.
  • THC Buffer: Prevents THC-induced tachycardia and paranoia by modulating how THC attaches to brain receptors.

3. CBG (Cannabigerol) — The Stem-Cell Cannabinoid

What It Is: The non-intoxicating precursor molecule from which all other cannabinoids (THC, CBD, CBC) are synthesized inside the living plant.

  • Antibacterial & Antimicrobial: Demonstrates exceptional efficacy against drug-resistant bacterial strains like MRSA.
  • Neuroprotective in Huntington's & Parkinson's: Protects striatal neurons and improves motor function in neurodegenerative models.
  • Gastrointestinal Healing: Reduces inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) flare-ups and colon inflammation.

4. CBN (Cannabinol) — The Natural Sleep & Sedative Compound

What It Is: Formed naturally as THC oxidizes over time. It exhibits mild psychoactivity with strong physical relaxation properties.

  • Sedation & Insomnia Relief: Acts as one of the most potent natural sedatives in botanical medicine, improving deep sleep architecture.
  • Bone Healing & Tissue Regeneration: Stimulates recruitment of bone marrow stem cells, aiding fracture recovery.
  • Antispasmodic: Relieves severe muscle spasms in Multiple Sclerosis and spinal injuries.

5. THCV (Tetrahydrocannabivarin) — The Metabolic Regulator

What It Is: A propyl-analog cannabinoid found in specific African sativa landraces.

  • Appetite Suppression & Weight Management: Blocks CB1 receptors at low doses, curbing cravings rather than inducing them.
  • Glycemic Control: Regulates blood sugar levels and improves insulin sensitivity in Type-2 diabetes research.

// TERPENE SYNERGY: THE ENTOURAGE EFFECT

Cannabis efficacy is not driven by cannabinoids alone. Aromatic terpenes dictate how effectively cannabinoids cross the blood-brain barrier:

Essential Terpene Profiles

  • Myrcene (Earthy/Musky): Increases cell membrane permeability in the brain, speeding up cannabinoid onset and providing deep body relaxation.
  • Beta-Caryophyllene (Peppery/Spicy): The only known terpene that functions directly as a CB2 receptor agonist, providing massive systemic anti-inflammatory relief.
  • Pinene (Pine/Forest): Opens airways (bronchodilator) and inhibits acetylcholinesterase, counteracting short-term memory impairment.
  • Limonene (Citrus): Boosts mood, relieves stress, and enhances cutaneous absorption of topical cannabinoid salves.
