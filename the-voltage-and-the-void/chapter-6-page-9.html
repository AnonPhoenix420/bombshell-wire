The walker's 7.62mm twin-barrel turret spun up with a dry, mechanical screech, pre-calculating their trajectory. Marcus didn't hesitate; he ripped a high-intensity thermal decoy flare from his web gear, cracked the primer against his thigh, and slammed it straight into the machine's primary optical array.

A blinding cascade of white-hot magnesium sparks erupted across the chassis. The machine stumbled blindly, its turret firing a deafening, erratic burst of heavy rounds into the canyon walls that showered them with razor-sharp rock splinters.

Marcus dragged Elena into the blinding, featureless expanse of the dry alkaline wash just as the automated killer lost their thermal trail in the glare.
