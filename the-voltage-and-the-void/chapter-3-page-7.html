CHAPTER 3: Ghost Vectors in the SCADA Stack

Location: The Upper Landing // Closing the Circle

Marcus scrambled backward across the soot-stained floor, his back hitting the cold concrete foundation wall. He needed to get out of the sub-basement before the thermal wave baked the stairwell into a chimney. He scrambled to his feet, gripping the terminal tightly, and sprinted back toward the concrete steps.

As he reached the landing, a wall of superheated air rolled down to meet him, carrying the metallic tang of vaporized solder and scorched paint. The wooden door at the top of the stairwell had warped in its steel frame, jammed shut by the extreme temperature differential.

He threw his shoulder against the warped wood once, twice, but it didn't budge. The frame had fused together under the intense heat.

He dropped to his knees, hauled out his terminal, and frantically ran a hardware scan using cyph3r to locate any nearby electro-mechanical actuators he could reverse-engineer.

# ./cyph3r --scan-actuators --interface local-rf --radius 50m
[+] Scanning local wireless and control buses...
    [>] Node 01: Automated Fire Suppression Vent (Status: LOCKED)
    [>] Node 02: Industrial Drainage Sluice Gate (Status: MANUAL OVERRIDE AVAILABLE)
[!] ROUTE DETECTED: Drainage conduit leads directly to harbor municipal overflow channel.

The drainage conduit. It was the only way out. The old stormwater discharge pipe ran from the back of the sub-basement straight out to the harbor seawall, bypassing the locked security gates and the burning upper floors.

He turned away from the jammed stairwell door and plunged deeper into the pitch-black recesses of the sub-basement, guided only by the eerie green glow of his terminal screen.
