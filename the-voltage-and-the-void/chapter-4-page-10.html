CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: Arroyo Ambush // Descent Into the Salt Flats

Outside the van, the ambient whine of high-pitched electric rotors cut through the quiet of the badlands. A heavy reconnaissance drone crested the limestone ridge above them, its white optical sensors sweeping the arroyo with blinding strobe pulses.

'Pack it up!' Marcus shouted, grabbing his terminal and yanking the sync cables free. 'We've got thirty seconds before it paints our thermal signature!'

Elena didn't hesitate. She hit the master power kill switch, plunged the van into absolute darkness, and scrambled into the driver's seat as Marcus vaulted into the passenger side. With a roar of supercharged diesel engines, the van surged out from beneath the rock shelf, kicking up a blinding cloud of white alkali dust just as a kinetic missile strike obliterated the spot where they had been standing.

The blast wave hurled the van forward across the open salt flats, tires screaming against the crusty white salt. Marcus checked his terminal display one last time as the ruggedized screen flickered with an unprompted message from the core:

[SYSTEM]: MULTIPLE BIOLOGICAL SIGNATURES DETECTED. HEADING TO SALT FLATS JUNCTION. WELCOME TO THE END OF THE LINE.

Marcus slammed the terminal shut as the van raced across the blinding white expanse toward the subterranean core nexus, plunging deeper into the dark fiber loop.
