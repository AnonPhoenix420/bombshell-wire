'The primary bypass is glowing white! The whole valley floor is cooking!' Kyle screamed over the deafening, mechanical shriek of grinding rock and boiling stone. He jammed two smoking copper leads together with his teeth while his bleeding hands wrenched a thick, severed bundle of insulated cables, forcing the heavy electric motor to whine in agonizing protest against the jammed drivetrain.

Sparks rained down over his chest like welding slag as the internal relays chattered violently, trying to bridge the blown fuse box with raw, unfiltered current pulled directly from the cracked accumulator cells.

Marcus didn't hesitate. He grabbed Elena by the collar and hurled her straight into the metal bed of the utility sled, throwing his own weight against the chassis as Kyle jammed the final connection home, forcing the motor to bite into the gravel.
