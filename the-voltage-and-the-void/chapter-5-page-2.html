CHAPTER 5: Subterranean Containment

Location: Black Ridge Approach // The Lead Bulkhead

Ahead, looming like a gray leviathan against the sharp sandstone cliffs of Black Ridge, was the sealed entrance to an abandoned federal research bunker. Constructed during the height of the nuclear armaments race and retrofitted decades later for quantum annealing experiments, the facility was wrapped in three feet of solid lead and high-density concrete shielding.

Elena swerved the van hard into the shadowed alcove of the bunker's access ramp, killing the engine and bringing the vehicle to a skidding halt right before the primary blast door.

Marcus tumbled out of the passenger door, his boots hitting the gravel, and rushed toward the manual override panel built into the reinforced steel frame.

# ./cyph3r --interface physical0 --bunker-override --deep-probe
[+] Interfacing with legacy pneumatic door controller...
    [>] Substation Relay: Cold-booting analog bus.
    [>] Shielding Check: Heavy lead matrix verified intact.
[!] SUCCESS: Blast door hydraulic locks disengaged.
[!] WARNING: [FLOCK‑NET] perimeter sentinel node detecting local movement.

The heavy pneumatic seals hissed, releasing forty years of stagnant, pressurized air as the twenty-ton bulkhead swung inward an inch at a time.
