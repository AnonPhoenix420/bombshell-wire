From the darkness inside, a pair of thick, heavily scarred hands reached out through the dark, snagging Elena by her vest and yanking her inside an instant before a second missile obliterated the outer apron.

Marcus dove headfirst through the narrow gap as the blast wave slammed the steel door shut behind him with the force of a naval cannon, rattling his teeth in his skull. He hit the cold steel catwalk, gasping for thick, stale air.

Above them, a massive, bearded figure threw his full weight onto a pneumatic locking lever. Four massive steel pins slammed home into the frame with resonant, earth-shaking thuds, sealing the bunker off from the radioactive hell outside.

'You're late, Vance,' Phoenix rasped from the gloom, holding a manual lever in one hand and a blood-stained surgical clamp in the other.

Phoenix dropped instantly to one knee beside Elena, slicing her tactical vest open with surgical shears as relentless burrowing munitions hammered the structural cap above. There was no turning back.
