Marcus dragged Elena up from the dirt, his vision swimming in a haze of red and black. He half-carried, half-dragged her across the smoking crater field as a second high-pitched acoustic whine screamed down from the drone swarm.

'Grab the wheel!' Marcus yelled, shoving Elena's hand onto the rusted manual override dial of the blast door while he threw his entire body weight against the massive three-ton steel plate.

Metal screeched against metal as the heavy door groaned open just wide enough for a body, revealing the pitch-black void inside.
