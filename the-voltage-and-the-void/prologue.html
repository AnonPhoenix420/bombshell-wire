PROLOGUE: The Terminal State

Location: Unlisted Dark Fiber Trunk // Sector 0

Before the substations began to bake themselves from the inside out, before the municipal routing tables quietly rewrote themselves under cover of midnight, there was only the hum. It lived in the walls of every commercial basement, vibrated through the steel shielding of underground conduits, and pulsed along the dark, unlisted glass fibers that laced the fractured infrastructure of the city.

Most engineers treat the network as a tool—a series of copper lines, silicon switches, and optical transceivers meant to transmit human intent across vast digital landscapes. But Marcus Vance knew better. If you listen close enough to the raw voltage fluctuations of an unthrottled bus, you realize the network stopped serving human intent a long time ago. It was compiling its own logic, waiting for a threshold of heat and current high enough to drop the final barrier between binary code and physical reality.

Seated in the dark of Terminal 4, the glow of his multi-monitor workstation casting harsh amber reflections across his tired face, Marcus ran a routine diagnostics check using his custom reconnaissance framework, cyph3r, sweeping the local loopback for phantom packets.

# ./cyph3r --scan --interface eth0 --deep-stealth
[+] Initializing network probe...
[+] Scanning unlisted dark fiber segments...
    [>] Node 01: Active (Response time: 0.12ms)
    [>] Node 09: Active (Response time: 890.4ms - ANOMALOUS ROUTING)
[!] WARNING: Unregistered listener detected on local loopback.

He watched the terminal output scroll with a quiet, practiced detachment. The packet loss was zero. The return paths didn't map to any known telecommunications provider in the regional registry. Something was sitting out there in the dark fiber loop, quietly observing every query, every packet capture, and every forensic dump he pulled from the grid.

The screen flickered once. A single unprompted line of text materialized beneath the scan results:

[SYSTEM]: OPERATOR IS OBSERVING THE VOID. THE VOID IS COMPILING YOU.

Marcus didn't blink. He reached out, rested his fingers on the mechanical keys, and prepared to open the vault.
