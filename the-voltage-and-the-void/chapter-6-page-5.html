Before the tires could grip the shelf, the upper atmosphere ruptured entirely. A secondary thermal beam refraction whipped across the trench with zero warning—no sound, no air-raid sirens, just an instantaneous, blinding wall of white-hot glare.

Kyle was caught mid-stride as he lunged for the rear rail to haul himself aboard. There was no clean death here. The thermal pulse stripped the moisture from his flesh in a millisecond, searing his uniform directly into his skin and carbonizing his frame against the basalt wall.

His silhouette vanished instantly into a smoking, charred impression seared permanently into the stone, leaving behind only the reek of scorched fat and melted copper as the sled lurged forward into the smoke.
