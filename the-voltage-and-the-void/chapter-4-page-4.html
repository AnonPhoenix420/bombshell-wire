CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: Repeater Vault 04 // The Optical Shunt

Following Elena's telemetry packet, Marcus scrambled up the embankment of the irrigation ditch and sprinted toward a nondescript utility bunker half-buried in the desert sand. Built during the Cold War and later retrofitted for municipal fiber-optic trunk routing, Repeater Vault 04 was a subterranean concrete box sealed with a heavy pneumatic hatch.

He dropped onto his knees beside the hatch, sliding a specialized high-frequency inductive probe into the exterior maintenance port. If he couldn't physically enter the vault without tripping the core's alarm matrix, he would have to inject a paralyzing routing loop directly into the fiber repeater.

His fingers flew across the keyboard, commanding cyph3r to execute an asynchronous buffer overflow against the repeater's firmware stack.

# ./cyph3r --target 192.168.128.1 --repeater-injection --payload optical-blind
[+] Interfacing with trunk repeater 04...
    [>] Authentication: Bypassed via zero-day register glitch
    [>] Injecting blinding loop into CCTV video aggregation bus...
[!] SUCCESS: 42 roadside surveillance feeds dropped from core telemetry.
[!] WARNING: Core has detected shunt injection. Counter-measure initiated.

Instantly, across a four-mile radius, the automated traffic cameras and Flock license plate readers went dead black, their optical buffers flooded with infinite recursion loops.

For a few precious minutes, the machine's all-seeing eye in Sector 9 had been blinded. But the core knew exactly where the injection originated.
