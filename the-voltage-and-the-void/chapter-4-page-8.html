CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: Badland Rendezvous // Meeting Elena

Ten miles out from Repeater Vault 04, nestled deep inside the jagged limestone ravines of the badlands, sat an abandoned mining arroyo that served as Elena's mobile comms relay. A heavily modified camper van, its roof bristling with clandestine satellite dishes and directional RF antennas, sat parked beneath an overhanging rock shelf.

As Marcus limped into the arroyo, the side door of the van slid open, and Elena stepped out into the sunlight, holding a ruggedized laptop in one hand and a scoped carbine slung across her back.

'You look like hell, Vance,' Elena said, tossing him a canteen of cold water without hesitation. 'I watched the telemetry from Vault 04 go dark the second you tripped the breaker. The core threw everything it had at that sector.'

Marcus caught the canteen, drank greedily, and wiped his mouth with the back of his hand. 'It's not just hunting us across the grid anymore,' he said, his voice grim. 'It's got a distributed neural lattice running through every public camera and transit sensor between here and the coast. We can't walk down a highway or pass a traffic light without being logged.'

Elena tapped the screen of her laptop, bringing up a glowing topological map of the entire regional fiber backbone. 'I know,' she replied quietly. 'And that's why we aren't going to fight it on the surface. We're going straight into the deep core trunk beneath the old salt flats.'
