The desert didn't breathe; it radiated. By the time Marcus and Elena reached the perimeter of the abandoned Titan II complex, the midday heat had baked the air into a shimmering, distorting mirage that dissolved the horizon line into a wash of pale copper and white. Miles of rusted chain-link fence, topped with loops of razor wire choked in desert dust, marked the boundary of a military ghost town long since forgotten by the defense contractors who built it.

Above them, the sky was a bruised, cloudless violet, sliced open by the faint, high-altitude glint of a low-earth-orbit surveillance platform passing silently overhead.

Marcus dropped to one knee behind a sun-bleached concrete culvert, pulling his handheld terminal from his tactical rig. The casing was hot to the touch, baking under the ambient furnace of the Arizona panhandle. He tapped the physical key-matrix, bringing up a localized spectrum analyzer to check for active microwave sweeps.

[SCANNING: 2.4GHz - 18.0GHz BAND]
SRC: ORBITAL_SAT_108 [LGM-25C RECON GRID]
PWR: -41dBm [ACTIVE THERMAL SWEEP]
VEC: AZIMUTH 142.6 ELEVATION 68.2
STATUS: LOCK ACQUIRED // DISPERSAL IMMINENT

'They're painting the sector,' Marcus muttered, his voice dry and raspy against the wind. 'Thermal sweeps are running on a fifteen-minute loop. When that bird clears the ridge, we have a four-minute window before the automated drone patrol sweeps the fence line.'
