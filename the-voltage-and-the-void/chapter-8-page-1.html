The heavy hydraulic blast doors of the Titan II silo slammed shut with a bone-jarring, concussive thud that rattled the fillings in Marcus's teeth. Just outside, an orbital DEW sweep had scorched the valley grass into white ash, leaving the air thick with the suffocating stench of vaporized silica and burning hydrocarbons.

Phoenix hauled Marcus and Elena inward, his boots skidding across the concrete dust as heavy alarms wailed overhead. He slammed his palm against the manual closure toggle, locking the massive steel pins into their housing just as a searing wall of thermal energy licked against the exterior seals.

Elena gasped in ragged agony, collapsing against a metal storage rack as white-hot pain lanced up her injured thigh. The glancing DEW strike from the valley sprint had charred her tactical fatigues and fused the fabric directly into her skin, leaving a raw, weeping burn that bled sluggishly onto the floor.

Marcus dropped beside her, tearing open an emergency trauma pack with trembling fingers. His hands were slick with cold sweat and soot, tasting copper and metallic bile in the back of his throat as he pressed a sterile compression dressing over her ruined leg.
