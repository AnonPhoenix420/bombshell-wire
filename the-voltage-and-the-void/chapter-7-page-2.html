CHAPTER 7 NAVIGATION (P.1 - P.16):

The heavy blast hatch groaned on its grease-caked hydraulic hinges, yielding to Marcus's pry bar with a sound like tearing sheet metal. A wall of stale, subterranean air rushed up to meet them—heavy with the dead weight of fifty-year-old hydraulic fluid, damp concrete, and absolute silence.

Elena swung her legs over the rim of the access shaft, her weight shifting onto her left side. A sharp, involuntary intake of breath hissed through her teeth. The ragged entry wound on her right thigh—stitched hastily in the back of a salt-flat runner van with coarse nylon thread—strained against the pressure of her tactical trousers, leaving a dark, spreading crescent of fresh crimson soaking through the fabric.

'I'm on the ladder,' she whispered, her voice bouncing off the cylindrical concrete walls of the descent tube. Her knuckles were stark white as she gripped the rusted rungs.

'Don't push the leg,' Marcus muttered, dropping down behind her into the shaft and pulling the heavy steel lid halfway shut to block the desert glare.
