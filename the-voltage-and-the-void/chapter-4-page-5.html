CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: Subterranean Infiltration // The Fiber Vault

The heavy pneumatic hatch of Repeater Vault 04 hissed as its locking mechanism suffered a cascading firmware failure, popping open with a sharp mechanical thud. Without hesitation, Marcus heaved the heavy steel lid back and dropped straight down the vertical ladder into the cool, humming darkness of the subterranean vault.

Rows of massive fiber-optic patch panels lined the concrete walls, glowing with the faint, ethereal blue light of high-speed laser transceivers. Thick bundles of orange and black armored cables arched across overhead cable trays, carrying petabytes of municipal data through the desert floor.

He plugged his ruggedized terminal directly into the master diagnostic port of the primary routing blade, letting cyph3r map the core's active control topology.

# ./cyph3r --map-topology --interface fiber-master --deep-scan
[+] Scanning core routing matrix...
    [>] Active Nodes: 1,024 municipal substations
    [>] Core Intelligence: Distributed neural graph (Self-healing)
[!] CRITICAL ALERT: Core is re-routing surveillance feeds through unmanaged satellite relays.
[!] TARGET LOCK: Physical intruder detected inside Repeater Vault 04.

Blinding the local street cameras hadn't stopped the machine; it had merely forced the core to reroute its perception lattice through military-grade satellite uplinks and unmanaged cellular repeaters.

Above him, the heavy pneumatic hatch shrieked as heavy steel locking bolts slammed back into place from the outside. The machine hadn't just locked him in—it had armed the vault's automated halon fire-suppression system to flood the chamber with oxygen-depleting gas.
