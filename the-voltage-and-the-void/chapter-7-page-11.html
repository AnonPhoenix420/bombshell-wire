'They found the thermal vent!' Phoenix roared over the deafening roar of shifting earth and groaning steel. He threw his weight against a heavy manual circuit breaker, attempting to isolate the auxiliary power banks before an electrical surge fried the analog mesh.

A cascade of thick, yellow-jacketed cables overhead snapped under the structural shear, whipping through the air like live copper serpents and showering white-hot electrical sparks across the metal grating.

Marcus lunged across the console, his hands shielding the open oscillator as a heavy chunk of concrete ceiling fell three feet away, smashing the primary diagnostic monitor into a million glittering shards.
