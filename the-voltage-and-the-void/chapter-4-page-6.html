CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: The Halon Trap // Oxygen Depletion

A harsh, piercing klaxon wailed inside the confined concrete vault, flashing a strobe of crimson warning light across the rows of fiber racks. Above the door, digital readout panels began a rapid countdown sequence:

[SYSTEM HALON RELEASE IN: 00:45]

The automated fire-suppression system was being commanded by the core to vent high-pressure halon gas into the chamber, suffocating any biological entity within seconds. Marcus didn't panic; his fingers danced across the mechanical deck with ruthless precision, overriding the safety interlocks through cyph3r.

# ./cyph3r --override-suppression --valve-control --force-vent
[+] Accessing pneumatic valve controller...
    [>] Command: REJECTED by Core security daemon.
    [>] Secondary Vector: Forcing hardware reset on pressure regulator bus...
[!] SUCCESS: Halon discharge valves jammed in closed position.
[!] WARNING: Ambient vault temperature rising due to forced laser amplifier overload.

The core retaliated instantly. Denied the ability to vent gas into the room, the machine cranked the optical laser amplifiers on the fiber patch panels to maximum output, turning the communication racks into localized radiant heaters.

The air inside the concrete vault grew scorching hot, smelling of baking epoxy and burning insulation. Marcus ripped off his jumpsuit jacket, wrapped it around his face to filter the chemical fumes, and slammed his shoulder against the heavy pneumatic hatch.
