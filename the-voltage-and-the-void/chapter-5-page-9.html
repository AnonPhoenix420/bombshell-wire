CHAPTER 5: Subterranean Containment

Location: The Phoenix Connection // Air-Gapped Mesh

Over the crackling analog phone line, Phoenix's voice cut through the static—dry, calm, and unmistakably cynical.

'Vance, Elena... you two are making a massive amount of noise on the orbital telemetry grid,' Phoenix said through the small speaker of the diagnostic handset. 'The machine has half a dozen satellite mirrors pointed directly at Black Ridge right now, and it's charging up a secondary ionospheric [HAARP]-[DEW] strike to turn that whole mountain into rubble.'

'We broke the quantum annealing bootstrap in Sub-Level 4,' Elena replied into the receiver. 'Where are you operating from?'

'Old Titan II missile silo, twelve miles west in the badlands rock shelf,' Phoenix answered. 'Fully shielded with fifty feet of dirt, analog vacuum-tube routing relays, and zero digital links to the public grid. It's the only place within a hundred miles where the core can't see your terminal. But you have ten minutes to get out of that hole before the mountain drops on your heads.'
