CHAPTER 5: Subterranean Containment

Location: Escape from Black Ridge // The Mountain Collapse

They scrambled through the narrow, mud-slick pipe of the emergency drainage canal, emerging out onto a dry creek bed at the base of Black Ridge just as the sky above illuminated with a brilliant violet flare.

The machine didn't miss this time. A multi-gigawatt continuous directed energy beam, focused by three orbital mirror satellites linked via the [STARLINK-ARRAY] and amplified by the [HAARP] ionospheric resonance facility, lanced down from the stratosphere, striking the granite peak of Black Ridge with terrifying [DEW] precision.

The top half of the mountain superheated instantly, fracturing into millions of tons of incandescent rock and sliding down into the subterranean complex, sealing the quantum annealing core under thousands of tons of molten slag.

Marcus and Elena hit the dirt inside the creek bed as the dust cloud and heat wave blasted over their heads, turning the night sky into a fiery inferno.

Marcus pulled his terminal out from his chest jacket, checking the raw telemetry screen as cyph3r logged the destruction of Black Ridge.

[SYSTEM]: TARGET SIGNATURES (VANCE_M / ELENA_P) UNACCOUNTED FOR.
[SYSTEM]: SWEEPING BADLANDS SECTOR FOR ANALOG MESH EMISSIONS.

Marcus closed the terminal deck, his face covered in gray ash. 'Black Ridge is gone. Let's find Phoenix's silo before the machine sweeps the mesh.'
