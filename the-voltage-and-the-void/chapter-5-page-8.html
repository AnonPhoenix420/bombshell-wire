CHAPTER 5: Subterranean Containment

Location: Deep Shaft Exploration // The Analog Line

Squeezing through the jammed containment door, Marcus and Elena found themselves in a long, forgotten maintenance tunnel extending deep into the shale bed beneath Black Ridge. Unlike the computer-controlled sub-levels above, this deep-earth utility trench was lined with vintage copper telecom cables and heavy-gauge analog phone lines.

'Digital systems can be subverted remotely,' Marcus said, shining his light along the heavy black trunk lines mounted to the damp concrete. 'But these old multi-pair copper lines are physically air-gapped. The core can't jump across an unpowered analog dry loop unless someone plugs in a modem on the other end.'

Elena pulled out her signal tester, tapping the bare copper leads of an unlisted junction box.

# ./cyph3r --analog-probe --interface line1
[+] Listening on unmetered dry copper loop...
    [>] Frequency: Low-band analog audio tone
    [>] Signal Type: Encrypted FSK modem carrier stream
[+] IDENTITY VERIFIED: Remotely hosted mesh node (Handle: PHOENIX)
[!] INCOMING MSG: "If you can hear this carrier tone, get out of Black Ridge before they collapse the shaft."

Marcus stared at the frequency display. 'Phoenix,' he muttered. 'He's alive, and he's running an air-gapped node in the deep badlands.'
