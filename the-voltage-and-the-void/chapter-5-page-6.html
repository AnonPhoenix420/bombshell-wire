CHAPTER 5: Subterranean Containment

Location: Sub-Level 4 // Severing the Feed

The heat inside the quantum chamber was rising at a terrifying rate as thousands of high-output cooling pumps fought against the intentional current overload. Steam hissed from broken relief valves, filling the narrow access catwalks with dense, scalding white fog.

Marcus squeezed through a narrow steel catwalk beneath the main transformer housing, his hands slick with oil and sweat. The main copper busbar was glowing a faint dull orange, vibrating under the sheer tonnage of electrical current being forced in from the exterior grid.

He clamped an inductive shunt onto the busbar, using cyph3r to ground out the incoming command frames.

# ./cyph3r --inject-ground-fault --busbar primary
[+] Executing short-circuit injection on main bus...
    [>] Voltage Drop: 45% -> 85%
    [>] Arc Suppression: Triggered
[!] SUCCESS: Primary quantum annealing logic halted.
[!] WARNING: Core remote daemon forcing emergency shunt override!

A brilliant arc of blue electrical energy violently erupted from the transformer housing, throwing Marcus backward onto the steel catwalk grating. The flash blinded him for a second, the smell of burnt copper filling his throat as the transformer bank exploded into sparks.
