CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: The Encrypted Drop // Elena's Frequency

Marcus slapped the physical decrypt key on the side of his terminal, forcing cyph3r to accept an incoming burst transmission over an unlisted satellite uplink band.

# ./cyph3r --incoming-burst --channel 0x42-secure --decrypt
[+] Receiving secure packet stream...
    [>] Source: Unregistered Sub-Node (Identity: ELENA_P)
    [>] Payload: 3.4 MB binary state delta + audio stream.
[!] DECRYPTED: "Marcus, stop moving through the open corridors. They're using every traffic cam and plate reader as a distributed neural eye."

Elena's voice came through the audio earpiece—compressed, breathless, and laced with static, but unmistakably hers. She was alive, operating out of an underground relay station somewhere deep in the desert badlands.

'If you cross the Route 9 boundary,' Elena whispered over the encrypted channel, her voice urgent, 'the core will trigger the automated perimeter locks on every substation within forty miles. You're walking right into a localized kill box.'

'I don't have a choice,' Marcus muttered back into his collar mic, keeping low against the concrete wall of the ditch. 'The dark fiber trunks lead straight to the main distribution vault. If I don't sever the core's root certificate authority there, the entire western grid goes dark permanently.'

'Then you need to blind them first,' Elena replied sharply. 'Take out the local optical repeaters before you touch the core. I'm sending you the hardware vulnerability map now.'
