They pushed out into the open basin, the salt crust crunching beneath their boots like shattered glass. The ion-storm overhead created an eerie, ambient luminescence—a pale, ghost-white glow that bounced off the endless white salt pans, making it impossible to tell where the ground ended and the sky began. The Glass Horizon.

Every step Elena took sent a white-hot spike of agony up her right thigh. The coarse nylon stitches were biting deep into inflamed muscle tissue, but she didn't utter a single sound, her eyes locked on the dark, jagged silhouette of the distant highway arroyo.

Marcus kept his arm locked firmly around her waist, half-carrying her forward while his free hand clutched the encrypted payload case tightly against his ribs.
