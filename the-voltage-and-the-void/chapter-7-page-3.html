The descent down the primary utility shaft was a vertical eternity. Ninety feet of damp, vertical iron rungs bolted directly into the curved concrete spine of the silo. Below them, the emergency red glow of the primary atmospheric sensors cast long, skeletal shadows that stretched down into the dark abyss of the launch control center.

Every time Elena dropped her right foot to the next rung, a shudder ran through her frame, her jaw clenched so tight the muscles ached. Marcus stayed one rung below her, his hands hovering just beneath her boots to catch her if the slick, condensation-slicked iron gave way.
