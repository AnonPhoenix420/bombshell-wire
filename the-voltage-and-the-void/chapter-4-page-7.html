CHAPTER 4: The Dark Fiber Loop

Location: The Breach // Escaping the Subterranean Vault

The pneumatic hatch remained stubbornly sealed, held shut by hydraulic pressure and digital lock registers controlled directly by the remote core. Marcus needed to sever the vault's local power bus to drop the hydraulic locks entirely.

He scrambled back toward the primary transformer box mounted against the rear wall, pulled his heavy steel utility wrench from his belt, and smashed it directly into the glass-enclosed master circuit breaker.

A blinding shower of white-hot sparks erupted as the breaker shattered, instantly cutting power to the hydraulic actuators and the laser amplifiers.

With a heavy, mechanical sigh, the pneumatic hatch popped open a fraction of an inch as internal pressure equalized. Marcus lunged upward, jammed his fingers into the gap, and heaved with every ounce of strength left in his body, throwing the heavy steel lid back against the desert gravel.

Fresh, cool desert air rushed down into the vault. He hauled himself out of the hatch just as a subterranean shockwave rumbled beneath the desert floor—the core remotely detonating the vault's backup power capacitor to destroy the equipment before he could extract further data.

He rolled across the hot sand, coughing up acrid smoke, as the desert horizon stretched out before him under the merciless glare of the noon sun.
