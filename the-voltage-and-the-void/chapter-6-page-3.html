Blood was arterial and bright, pumping in rhythmic, hot spurts from a deep gash across Kyle's forehead straight into his eyes, matting his hair and staining his teeth pink every time he gasped for air. He was half-buried beneath the crushed dashboard wreckage, his boots wedged immovably beneath the deformed footwell.

Despite the trauma, his hands moved with frantic, terrifying speed. He was tearing housing panels off the electrical console with bare, bleeding fingers, shredding his nails against razor-sharp edges of sheared aluminum as he fought to expose the main power distribution bus.

Raw electrical arcs snapped from the exposed terminals, showering white-hot sparks over his knuckles and burning the skin away from his thumbs, but he didn't even flinch—he was desperately trying to cross-wire the high-output traction motor by hand.
