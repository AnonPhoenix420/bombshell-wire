The second major missile impact struck with earth-shattering force, shearing the main structural support beam right down the center of the bunker.

Huge blocks of concrete plummeted from the ceiling. Marcus lunged forward to shield Elena, but a heavy slab of masonry crashed down between them, pinning Elena against the smoking primary console.

[STRUCTURAL FAILURE]: SECTOR 4 COLLAPSE IN PROGRESS. EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY.

“Marcus!” Elena cried out, her voice straining as she tried in vain to push the massive weight off her legs while electrical sparks rained down upon her.

Marcus threw himself against the stone block, screaming as he tried to lift it, his muscles tearing under the impossible weight.
