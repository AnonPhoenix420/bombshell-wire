The truck bounced violently across cracked potholes and fractured asphalt, its worn-out shock absorbers bottoming out with bone-jarring crunches against the frame. Above them, the drones closed the distance, their twin-barrel turrets spinning up with a dry, mechanical whir.

A burst of heavy kinetic rounds chewed up the asphalt ten feet behind the tailgate, sending a shower of razor-sharp black shards flying into the truck bed.

'Hold on!' Marcus yelled, wrenching the steering wheel hard to the left as they careened off the maintenance road and plunged directly down a steep, rocky arroyo wash.
