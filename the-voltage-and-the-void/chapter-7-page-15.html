Through the ringing in his ears and the blinding red strobe lights, Marcus crawled back to the console on hands and knees. The screen was completely dead, charred black by the thermal back-blast.

He stared frantically at the status indicator LED on the hardline trunk interface. For one agonizing second, it flickered dark. Then, with a soft, mechanical click, it flashed solid green.

Complete. The data archive had cleared the silo boundary and vanished into the analog wilderness before the secondary blast could seal the vault forever.
