CHAPTER 5: Subterranean Containment

Location: DEW Ignition // The Beam Strike

Above the ridge, the sky didn't turn dark—it turned an unnatural, shimmering blue. The atmospheric resonance generated by the remote [HAARP] ionospheric arrays focused a dense pillar of ionized air directly above the salt flats, creating an optical conduit for a space-based directed energy weapon.

'Marcus! Inside, now!' Elena screamed, grabbing her equipment bag and sprinting toward the opening bulkhead.

Marcus scrambled through the narrow gap in the steel blast doors just as a silent, blinding laser pulse struck the dry earth fifty yards behind their abandoned van. There was no thunder, no explosive fireball—just instantaneous, searing heat. The sand melted into a pool of glowing green glass, and the van's steel chassis collapsed into white-hot liquid metal within seconds.
