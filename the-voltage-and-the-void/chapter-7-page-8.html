With Elena stabilized on the cot, Marcus turned his attention back to the primary console. To punch through the orbital jamming grid, they couldn't broadcast a standard TCP/IP handshake. They had to construct a cascading analog frequency hop—bouncing raw electrical pulses through the abandoned military telephone lines buried deep beneath the Arizona caliche.

He wired a heavy copper jumper cable from the vacuum-tube oscillator directly into the silo's legacy hardline trunk, his fingers flying across the rotary dial as he punched in the cold-war bootstrap sequence.

The vacuum tubes hummed in unison, their internal glass envelopes growing hot to the touch as they drew massive surges of raw current from the bunker's emergency diesel generators.
