INTEL BRIEF: SHALERSVILLE ZONING AMENDMENTS & DATA CENTER PUSH

Published: September 18, 2026 - Wendy DiAlesandro | Classification: Local Government & Regulatory Audit

Shalersville’s Zoning Commission on Sept. 10 approved a handful of amendments to the township’s zoning code that could affect the contentious proposed data center campus.

The commission added any use “that may create occasional, objectionable or hazardous elements such as smoke, noise, humming vibration, odor or dust observed beyond the property line, and having some outside storage, or capable of using or generating power at a rate greater than or equal to 25 megawatts” to its definition of heavy industrial usage.

Shalersville does not currently have any land zoned for heavy industrial usage. The data center proposed by GEIS Companies and Bitdeer Technologies Group would draw 750 megawatts, well beyond the 50 megawatt benchmark Ohio law defines as a major utility facility.

1. Who is GIS? (Geis Companies & Stonemont Financial)

Founded in Ohio in 1967, Geis Companies (GIS) has evolved into one of the largest privately held, vertically integrated real estate development, design-build, and construction firms in the Midwest. Partnering alongside Atlanta-based Stonemont Financial Group, they developed the 470-acre master-planned Turnpike Commerce Center located off Route 44 and Beck Road adjacent to the Ohio Turnpike.

2. Who is Bitdeer? (Global Footprint & Function)

Headquartered in Singapore, Bitdeer Technologies Group is a premier global technology company operating at the bleeding edge of blockchain, high-performance computing (HPC), and artificial intelligence (AI) cloud infrastructure. Operating massive power capacity and physical data center sites across North America, Europe, and Asia, Bitdeer commands tens of gigawatts/megawatts of electrical capacity to fuel industrial-scale blockchain verification and AI model training.

3. The Shalersville Data Center Project Overview

Bitdeer's proposed campus at the Turnpike Commerce Center in Shalersville Township is slated to become one of the company's largest infrastructure plays in the United States. Key project specifications include:

  • 📍 Location & Scope: Situated on approximately 257 acres of the Geis-developed Turnpike Commerce Center along Beck Road.
  • ⚡ Power Capacity: Designed with an initial electrical capacity scaling up to an astonishing 750 megawatts.
  • 🏗️ Infrastructure Design: Proposed as a massive multi-building campus featuring compact, high-efficiency data halls configured specifically for high-density AI and crypto-mining hardware.
