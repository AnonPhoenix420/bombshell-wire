INTEL BRIEF: SHALERSVILLE ZONING AMENDMENTS & DATA CENTER PUSH

Published: September 18, 2026 - Wendy DiAlesandro | Classification: Local Government & Regulatory Audit

Shalersville’s Zoning Commission on Sept. 10 approved a handful of amendments to the township’s zoning code that could affect the contentious proposed data center campus.

The commission added any use “that may create occasional, objectionable or hazardous elements such as smoke, noise, humming vibration, odor or dust observed beyond the property line, and having some outside storage, or capable of using or generating power at a rate greater than or equal to 25 megawatts” to its definition of heavy industrial usage.

Shalersville does not currently have any land zoned for heavy industrial usage. The data center proposed by GEIS Companies and Bitdeer Technologies Group would draw 750 megawatts, well beyond the 50 megawatt benchmark Ohio law defines as a major utility facility.

1. Who is GIS? (Geis Companies & Stonemont Financial)

Geis Companies is a prominent regional developer known for large-scale industrial parks and master-planned logistics hubs across Northeast Ohio. Partnering alongside Stonemont Financial Group, they manage land acquisition, speculative building, and structural execution for major corporate logistics and technological campuses.

2. Who is Bitdeer? (Global Footprint & Function)

Bitdeer Technologies Group is a Singapore-based cryptocurrency mining and high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure provider. Operating facilities across North America and Europe, they specialize in multi-megawatt server loads designed to support intensive blockchain verification and artificial intelligence scaling.

3. The Shalersville Data Center Project: Factual Breakdown

The proposed development targets approximately 257 acres within Portage County’s Turnpike Commerce Center corridor. Key infrastructure parameters include:

  • Power Consumption: Designed for an initial and scaling capacity reaching up to 750 megawatts.
  • Physical Scale: Master-planned for up to 15 modular multi-story server buildings.
  • Development Phases: Divided into distinct construction blocks pushing outward from Beck Road.
