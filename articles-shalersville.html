INTEL BRIEF: SHALERSVILLE ZONING AMENDMENTS & DATA CENTER PUSH

Published: September 18, 2026 - Wendy DiAlesandro | Classification: Local Government & Regulatory Audit

Shalersville’s Zoning Commission on Sept. 10 approved a handful of amendments to the township’s zoning code that could affect the contentious proposed data center campus.

The commission added any use “that may create occasional, objectionable or hazardous elements such as smoke, noise, humming vibration, odor or dust observed beyond the property line, and having some outside storage, or capable of using or generating power at a rate greater than or equal to 25 megawatts” to its definition of heavy industrial usage.

Shalersville does not currently have any land zoned for heavy industrial usage. The data center proposed by GEIS Companies and Bitdeer Technologies Group would draw 750 megawatts, well beyond the 50 megawatt benchmark Ohio law defines as a major utility facility.
