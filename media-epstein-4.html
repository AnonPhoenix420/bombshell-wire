// FEED 04: Federal Bureau of Prisons Oversight Breaches & Inspector General Findings

Forensic Overview: A rigorous breakdown of Department of Justice Office of the Inspector General (OIG) Report 23-085, examining the systemic operational failures leading up to the August 2019 death within the Special Housing Unit (SHU) of the Metropolitan Correctional Center (MCC) in New York.

Investigative Context: The OIG investigation uncovered widespread institutional negligence, including broken security camera systems, falsified correctional officer logbooks, severe staff shortages, and blatant violations of federal detention facility operating procedures. This video feed and breakdown analyze how bureaucratic failure and administrative breakdown compromised high-profile federal custody security.
