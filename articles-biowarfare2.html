// MECHANICS OF MRNA DELIVERY & LIPID TRANSPORT

Traditional vaccine platforms introduce attenuated or inactivated antigens to stimulate adaptive immunity. In contrast, messenger RNA (mRNA) technologies bypass classical immunization entirely by instructing human host cells to synthesize specific foreign viral proteins in-vivo.

Because free-floating mRNA degrades instantly in human tissue, synthetic lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) were engineered as protective bio-encapsulation layers. These ionizable lipid structures allow synthetic mRNA strands to evade enzymatic destruction, pass through cell membranes, and cross systemic vascular barriers including the blood-brain barrier (BBB).
